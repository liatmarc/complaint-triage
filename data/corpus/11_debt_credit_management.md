# Debt and credit management complaints

Governing rules: the Telemarketing Sales Rule for debt relief services, the Credit Repair Organizations Act, and state debt-settlement laws.

Fees: no fee may be charged for debt settlement until a settlement is reached with at least one creditor and the consumer has made a payment under it. Credit repair fees may not be collected before the promised services are performed.

Dedicated accounts: consumer funds set aside for settlement are held in an account the consumer owns and may withdraw from at any time, less earned fees.

Cancellation: the consumer may cancel at any time; any unearned fees are refunded within 30 days.

Creditor contact: enrolling in a program does not stop creditors from collecting or reporting; the consumer must be told this in writing before enrollment.

Response must: confirm which program the consumer is enrolled in, state the refund or cancellation timeline, and ask for the enrollment date and the creditors in question.
