# Credit card complaints

Governing rules: Truth in Lending Act, Fair Credit Billing Act, and Regulation Z.

Billing errors: the consumer must write to us within 60 days of the statement date showing the error. We acknowledge in writing within 30 days and resolve within two billing cycles (no more than 90 days). During the investigation the consumer need not pay the disputed amount or related finance charges, and we may not report it as delinquent.

Unauthorized charges: consumer liability is capped at $50 under federal law; Northbridge policy is zero liability for reported unauthorized charges.

Interest rate increases: 45 days advance notice is required, and increases do not apply to existing balances except after 60 days of delinquency.

Payments: payments received by 5 p.m. on the due date are on time. Late fees are capped by regulation; a first late fee that exceeds the cap is refunded on request.

Merchant disputes (chargebacks): a dispute for goods not received or not as described is opened when the consumer provides the transaction date, amount, merchant name, and a summary of their attempt to resolve it with the merchant. Provisional credit is applied while the merchant responds, usually within 45 days.

Response must: confirm whether the issue is a billing error, an unauthorized charge, or a merchant dispute; state the acknowledgment (30 days) and resolution (two billing cycles) timelines; and ask for the statement date and transaction details.
