# Mortgage complaints

Governing rules: Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) and Regulation X; Truth in Lending Act for disclosures.

Written requests: a complaint that identifies the loan and the error is treated as a Notice of Error or Request for Information. We acknowledge within 5 business days and respond within 30 business days (extendable by 15 business days with notice), except payoff-amount requests, which are answered within 7 business days.

Payments: a full periodic payment must be credited the day it is received. Partial payments may be held in suspense; the consumer must be told this on their statement.

Escrow: an annual escrow analysis explains changes in the monthly payment. Shortages may be spread over 12 months. If the consumer disputes the analysis, we recompute and send the worksheet.

Loss mitigation: a complete application received more than 37 days before a foreclosure sale must be evaluated within 30 days for all available options. We may not make the first foreclosure filing until the loan is more than 120 days delinquent, and not while a complete application is pending.

Servicing transfers: both the old and new servicer must send notice, and payments sent to the old servicer within 60 days of transfer cannot be treated as late.

Response must: confirm the complaint is logged as a Notice of Error or Request for Information, state the 5-business-day acknowledgment and 30-business-day response timeline, and ask for the loan number (last four digits) and the payment dates in question.
