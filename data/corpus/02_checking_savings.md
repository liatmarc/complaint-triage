# Checking and savings account complaints

Governing rules: Electronic Fund Transfer Act and Regulation E for electronic transactions; Expedited Funds Availability Act and Regulation CC for deposit holds.

Unauthorized transactions (Reg E): the consumer must notify us within 60 days of the statement showing the error. We must investigate within 10 business days; if we need longer, we may take up to 45 days but must provisionally credit the disputed amount within 10 business days. For new accounts (under 30 days old) the limits are 20 business days and 90 days. Consumer liability is capped at $50 if they report within 2 business days of learning of a lost card, and $500 if within 60 days.

Deposit holds (Reg CC): the first $225 of most deposits is available the next business day; the remainder normally by the second business day. Extended holds (up to 7 business days) require a written notice giving the reason.

Overdraft fees: a consumer must have opted in to overdraft coverage for one-time debit card and ATM transactions before we can charge an overdraft fee on those. If no opt-in is on file, the fees are refunded.

Account closures: we may close an account, but we must return the remaining balance to the consumer. If the account was closed for suspected fraud, the funds are released after the review, normally within 30 days.

Response must: confirm the dispute is opened, state the provisional credit timeline (10 business days) where applicable, and ask for the transaction dates and amounts in question.
