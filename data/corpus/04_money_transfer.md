# Money transfer, virtual currency and money service complaints

Governing rules: Regulation E for peer-to-peer and electronic transfers; the Remittance Transfer Rule (Reg E Subpart B) for international transfers.

Peer-to-peer transfers (app-based): if the consumer authorized a payment to a scammer, that is generally not an "unauthorized" transfer under Reg E and provisional credit is not required; we still open a case, attempt recovery from the recipient's institution, and explain the outcome. If the consumer did not authorize the transfer (account takeover, stolen device), the Reg E timelines apply: investigation within 10 business days, provisional credit within 10 business days, up to 45 days total.

International remittances: the consumer has 180 days from the promised delivery date to report an error. We investigate within 90 days and, if an error occurred, refund or resend at no extra cost.

Account holds and frozen balances: funds may be held for compliance review. We must tell the consumer the review is in progress and normally release or explain within 30 days. Holds cannot be indefinite.

Virtual currency: purchases and sales are final once executed; complaints about price movement are not errors. Complaints about failed withdrawals are treated as transfer errors above.

Response must: distinguish authorized-but-induced payments from unauthorized transfers, state the applicable timeline, and ask for the transaction ID, date, amount, and recipient details.
