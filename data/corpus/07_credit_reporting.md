# Credit reporting complaints

Governing rules: Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) and Regulation V.

Disputes sent to Northbridge as the furnisher: we investigate and respond within 30 days of receiving the dispute (45 days if the consumer sends additional information during the first 30). If the information is inaccurate or cannot be verified, we correct or delete it with every bureau we reported it to, and confirm the outcome to the consumer in writing.

Disputes sent to a bureau: the bureau forwards the dispute to us; the same 30-day clock applies from when the bureau received it.

Identity theft: a consumer who provides an identity theft report and a statement that the account is not theirs gets the tradeline blocked within 4 business days of the bureau receiving the report; we stop furnishing that account.

Accuracy: accounts must be reported with the correct balance, status, and date of first delinquency. A paid collection may still be reported as paid, but must not show a balance.

Response must: confirm the dispute is opened, state the 30-day investigation timeline, and ask the consumer to identify the specific tradeline and what they believe is inaccurate.
