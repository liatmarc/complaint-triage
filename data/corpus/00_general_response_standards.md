# General response standards (Northbridge Financial response playbook)

Northbridge Financial is a fictional institution; this playbook is illustrative.

Every first response to a consumer complaint must:
1. Acknowledge the specific issue the consumer raised, in plain language, without restating their whole complaint back to them.
2. State what Northbridge will do next and by when. Use the timelines in the product-specific playbook pages; never promise a shorter timeline than policy allows.
3. Tell the consumer exactly what, if anything, we need from them (documents, dates, account identifiers), and how to send it.
4. Give a case reference number and a direct contact channel.
5. Avoid admitting fault or legal liability in the first response. Use neutral language such as "we are reviewing" and "we will investigate" rather than "we made an error."
6. Never request a full Social Security number, full account number, or password in writing. Ask for the last four digits only.
7. Keep the response under 250 words, with a warm but professional tone. Do not use exclamation marks.

Escalation: complaints alleging discrimination, threats of legal action, or harm to a vulnerable consumer are escalated to the Complaints Review Team on the same business day.
