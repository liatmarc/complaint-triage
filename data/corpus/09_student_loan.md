# Student loan complaints

Governing rules: Higher Education Act and Department of Education servicing requirements for federal loans; Truth in Lending Act and state servicing laws for private loans.

Income-driven repayment (IDR): a complete application is processed within 10 business days; if income documentation is missing we request it once and place the account in administrative forbearance so no payment is missed while we process. Payments made under an approved plan count toward forgiveness.

Payment application: payments are applied first to fees, then interest, then principal, across loans in proportion unless the consumer directs otherwise. Extra payments are applied to the loan the consumer chooses.

Public Service Loan Forgiveness: we count qualifying payments on request and provide the count in writing within 30 days.

Credit reporting: a federal loan is reported as delinquent only after 90 days past due. Errors in reported status are corrected with the bureaus within 30 days.

Servicing transfers: the consumer receives notice from both servicers, and autopay must be re-enrolled with the new servicer; payments sent to the old servicer within 60 days are forwarded.

Response must: identify whether the issue is IDR processing, payment application, forgiveness counts, or reporting; state the relevant timeline; and ask for the loan account number (last four digits) and the date of the application or payment in question.
