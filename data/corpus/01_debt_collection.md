# Debt collection complaints

Governing rules: Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA) and Regulation F.

Validation: if the consumer disputes the debt or asks who the original creditor is within 30 days of receiving the validation notice, collection activity pauses until we mail verification of the debt. If the consumer's complaint is the first time we hear a dispute, treat the complaint as the dispute and pause collection while we verify.

Verification package: name of the original creditor, the account number (last four digits), the itemized balance, and the date of the last payment. Target: mail within 30 days of the dispute.

Contact rules: no calls before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. consumer local time, no more than seven call attempts in seven days per debt, and no contact at work if the consumer has said their employer prohibits it. If the consumer says in writing to stop contacting them, all contact stops except to confirm that or to notify of a specific legal action.

Credit reporting: if the debt is disputed, it must be reported as disputed to the credit bureaus. If the debt is verified as not belonging to the consumer, request deletion from all three bureaus within 30 days.

Response must: confirm the debt is on hold pending verification, state the 30-day verification timeline, and ask the consumer for any documents showing the debt was paid, settled, or belongs to someone else.
