# Prepaid card complaints

Governing rules: Regulation E prepaid account rules.

Registration: Reg E error-resolution protections apply once the card is registered. If a consumer reports an error on an unregistered card, we ask them to register and then handle the dispute under the standard timelines.

Errors and unauthorized transactions: investigation within 10 business days, provisional credit within 10 business days, up to 45 days total, with the same 60-day reporting window from the statement date as a checking account.

Fees: the short-form and long-form fee disclosures were provided at purchase. A complaint about an inactivity fee is answered by resending the disclosure and, at Northbridge, waiving the first such fee.

Frozen or locked cards: a card may be locked for suspected fraud; the consumer is contacted and the card unlocked or replaced within 5 business days once identity is confirmed.

Replacement cards: a lost or stolen card is replaced within 7 to 10 business days; the balance transfers to the new card.

Response must: confirm the card's registration status, state the 10-business-day provisional credit timeline, and ask for the transaction dates and amounts.
