# Vehicle loan and lease complaints

Governing rules: Truth in Lending Act (loans), Consumer Leasing Act and Regulation M (leases), state repossession law, and the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act for active-duty borrowers.

Repossession: we may repossess after default as the contract defines it, without breaching the peace. After repossession the consumer receives a written notice of the sale, the right to reinstate or redeem, and after the sale an accounting of the proceeds and any deficiency or surplus. A vehicle that was declared a total loss and paid by insurance must not be reported as a repossession; if it was, request correction with the bureaus within 30 days.

GAP and insurance payoffs: when an insurer pays a total-loss claim, the remaining balance is billed to GAP coverage if present. The consumer is not billed for the gap while the GAP claim is pending.

Payment application: payments are applied to interest accrued, then principal, unless the contract says otherwise. Extra payments are applied to principal on request.

Title release: after payoff, the lien release or title is sent within 10 business days, or the state's shorter deadline.

Leases: excess wear and mileage charges must follow the lease's disclosed schedule; the consumer may obtain an independent inspection before turn-in.

Response must: identify whether the issue is repossession, payoff/title, GAP, or payment application; state the correction or release timeline; and ask for the account number (last four digits) and the relevant dates.
